Four small things, every week

  1. Empty your head

    Write things down the moment they show up. No folders to choose first.

  2. Pick one step for today

    Each day, choose one thing you can start in two minutes.

  3. Look back each week

    Two short questions show what got done and where you got stuck.

  4. Keep going

    Send your week to one person you trust. It is easier to continue when someone sees it.

Why it's different

  • Nothing to set up

    No blank canvas and no system to learn first. The structure is ready. Two minutes in, you're using it.

  • Your files stay yours

    In the Mac app, what you write is saved as plain files on your own computer. If you stop using Echo, they are still there.

  • AI that doesn't guess

    AI is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, it is told to quote your own words, not to guess.

Where things stand

Echo is a Mac app today, and its maker uses it every day. A version for everyone is being built, and some parts described on this page aren't finished yet. Phone or computer first? The list decides, which is why the form asks.
