Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Who is responsible

Vincent Lu, the Netherlands. Contact: vincentluting@gmail.com.

What is collected when you join the list

Your email address, and your answer to “Where would you use it most?” if you give one.

Also one label, filled in for you and visible in the form, that helps decide what to build first: how you found this page (for example “linkedin”), your device type, your operating system, and the page language. You can delete it.

Why, and on what basis

To tell you when early access opens, and to decide which version to build first.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give by sending the Google form. You can withdraw it at any time.

What this site does not do

No cookies, no advertising trackers, no selling or sharing of your data. This site does not count visits. The form itself is a Google page: Google may use cookies there and its own privacy policy applies.

What is stored in your browser

One label saying how you found this page. It lives in session storage and disappears when you close the tab.

Who handles the data

The form is made with Google Forms, and the answers are kept in my own Google account.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security.

How long it is kept

Until early access has opened and you have been told, or until you ask for deletion, whichever comes first. Never longer than 24 months.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time by writing to vincentluting@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
